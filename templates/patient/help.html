The model agreement line matters as much as the percentage. When all five models agree, the result is more dependable. When they split roughly down the middle, treat the number with caution and seek a professional opinion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does it ask for two blood pressure numbers?

A blood pressure reading always has two parts, written as 120/80. The first is systolic and must always be the larger of the two. If you enter them the wrong way round the form will stop you, because the models were never trained on impossible readings and would return a meaningless answer.

How accurate is it?

The models score around 73% accuracy with a ROC AUC near 0.80 on 12,965 patients held back from training. That is useful for triage but is not a diagnosis. The system only sees eight routine measurements — no ECG, no family history, no blood test panel.

Who can see my results?

Patients see only their own assessments. Doctors and administrators can see all records so they can provide clinical oversight. Every action taken by staff is written to an audit log.

Can it replace a doctor?

No. It is a screening aid built as a student project. It cannot examine you, does not know your history, and can be wrong in both directions. Always confirm any result with a qualified doctor.
